The Pre-Authorized Code Flow enables users to receive digital credentials in their EUDI Wallet through a simple QR code scan. The issuer generates a QR code containing a credential offer and a pre-authorized code, which the user scans with their wallet. The wallet then securely connects to the issuer, redeems the pre-authorized code, and retrieves the verifiable credential without requiring a browser-based authorization flow. Once validated and issued, the credential is securely stored in the user's wallet, providing a seamless, secure, and user-friendly credential issuance experience.
